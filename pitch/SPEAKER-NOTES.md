# GradeSight — Speaker Notes

Use these as a script when walking a contractor / foreman through the deck. Keep it
conversational. Construction guys hate slides — keep moving, point at the AR mockups, get
to the dollar slide fast.

> **Tone:** Direct. No tech jargon. If they ask "how does it work" — answer with what
> they'll *see and do*, not how the cloud works.

---

## 1. Cover — "Dig it right. The first time."
Big handshake. One sentence:
> "This puts your survey points and grade depth right in the operator's eyes —
> survey-grade, no flags, no tape, no guessing."

Then: "Mind if I take 10 minutes to show you?"

## 2. The Problem
Read the bullets out loud. They'll nod at every one. Land the hammer:
> "One re-grade on a riffle/pool — eight grand, easy. That's not the worst case,
> that's *Tuesday*."

## 3. The Fix
> "Three states. On grade — hold. Too high — dig. Too deep — fill. The arrow always
> points the way the bucket needs to move. Operators get it in five minutes."

## 4. How It Works
> "We don't sell you GPS. You already have an RTK — Leica, Trimble, whatever — we
> use it. Bluetooth pair, done."

If they ask about RTK accuracy → point to the ⚠ warning bullet. **Win the trust.**

## 5. AR: Approach
Walk them around to your screen. Point at the HUD overlay.
> "He just walked up to the next dig point. The arrow says 4 meters straight ahead,
> 12 o'clock. He's done 3, has 9 to go. The label 'Pool entrance' came from the design file."

## 6. AR: Too High
> "He started digging. He's 18 cm above target. The arrow says down — that means
> bring the bucket down, take more out. Voice cue says 'dig eighteen centimeters'
> so he doesn't even have to look up."

## 7. AR: On Grade — *the money slide*
This is where the "ah-ha" lands.
> "Δ +0.012 means he's 12 millimeters above target. Within 5 cm tolerance. Ding.
> Done. He short-presses the side button and the glasses jump to the next point."

Pause. Let them imagine it.

## 8. AR: Too Deep + Quality Warning
> "Sometimes RTK gets sketchy — trees, weather, whatever. Instead of letting him
> dig to a bad number, the glasses warn him. That alone is worth the price."

## 9. As-Built CSV
> "End of day, you've got a CSV with every dig: target vs. actual, accuracy, time,
> location. Drop it in the binder. NRCS 580 closeout writes itself."

For permitting-heavy clients, **dwell here**. This is huge for them.

## 10. Pricing
Don't apologize. Read the numbers.
> "$1,495 per kit, one time. $99 per operator per month. Pause it any month you don't
> use it. Crew of 5 is $399 a month — works out to about eighty bucks an operator.
> And if you've got 25 operators or more, the whole company is $2,500 a month flat —
> unlimited."

**The enterprise hook (use it for big-company conversations):**
> "Look, at 25 operators per-seat would be $2,475/month — we're already capped at
> $2,500 right there. At 50 operators you're saving $2,450 a month versus per-seat.
> At 100 you're saving over seven grand a month. The bigger you grow, the more
> sense it makes."

Don't lead with the enterprise tier on small contractors — it confuses the easy
yes. Save it for when someone says "we've got a few crews running."

Hit the badges:
> "30 days free on every new kit. No multi-year contracts. We're not a Trimble service plan."

## 11. ROI
The kill shot. Walk through the table:
> "Year one per operator: $2,683. One avoided re-grade pays for the kit AND the year
> AND leaves five grand on the table. Conservative season savings — twenty grand."

## 12. Get Started
> "Pilot is one kit, one operator, 30 days. We come out for the first day. If it
> doesn't earn its keep, send it back."

Then shut up and let them talk.

## Common objections + answers

**"My guys won't wear glasses all day."**
> "They don't have to — operators put them on when they're working a marker.
> They're lighter than safety glasses. Most guys forget they have them on."

**"What if they break?"**
> "1-year warranty. We swap any kit that breaks under normal use. Hard case is in
> the box — they live in there between jobs."

**"We already have a Trimble GCS."**
> "Great — that's machine-mounted, $40k+, locked to one piece of iron. GradeSight
> is per-operator, $99/month, works on shovels and rakes too. Different tool."

**"What if RTK goes out?"**
> "Glasses warn you with a yellow ⚠. You stop until it comes back, or you fall
> back to your old method. We don't lie to you about a fix you don't have."

**"Can I see one in action?"**
> "Yes. Day on your jobsite, we bring a kit. If it doesn't deliver, we leave."

- "Synergy" (please)

## Do say

- "Survey-grade"
- "RTK fix quality"
- "Pays for itself"
- "We use the GPS you already have"
- "Pause the subscription any month you don't use it"

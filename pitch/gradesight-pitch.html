GradeSight03

Three steps. That's it.

How it works in the field.

1

Upload your dig points

Drop in a CSV or GeoJSON from your survey or design file. Each point has a target depth. Done.

2

Pair the RTK receiver

Your existing Leidos / Trimble / Topcon RTK pairs over Bluetooth. We don't sell you new GPS — we use yours.

3

Dig until it dings

Operator wears the glasses, walks the trench, and runs the bucket. The HUD calls out depth in real time.

What about RTK accuracy? GradeSight reads your RTK fix quality. If it drops to float, you get a ⚠ warning so you don't pour bad data into the ground. We trust the same fix your surveyor trusts.

GradeSight05

What the operator sees · 2 of 4

Too high? Dig more.

✗ ↓ TOO HIGH M04 · 3/12
↓ DIG 18 cm (+0.18 m)

Too high — dig down

  • ↓ — arrow points the way the bucket needs to move
  • DIG 18 cm — exactly how much more to take out
  • Updates 4× per second as the bucket moves
  • Glasses are monochrome green, so we use big symbols, not red/green colors
Voice cue: "Dig eighteen centimeters." Hands stay on the joysticks. Eyes stay on the trench.

On grade. DING.

✓ ON GRADE M04 · 3/12
Δ +0.012 m HOLD
short-press = mark done
🔔 DING

You hit it

  • ✓ ON GRADE — within ±5 cm of design depth
  • Δ +0.012 m — you're 12 mm above. Within tolerance. Stop.
  • A short two-tone ding plays — you don't even have to look up to know
  • Short-press the button — marks it done, glasses jump to the next point

That single sound saves the operator from second-guessing every scoop. They learn it in 5 minutes.

GradeSight07

What the operator sees · 4 of 4

Went too deep? Fill it.

✗ ↑ TOO DEEP M04 · 3/12
↑ FILL 12 cm (-0.12 m)
⚠ vAcc ±8 cm

Too deep — bring it up

  • ↑ FILL 12 cm — backfill or grade up that much
  • ⚠ vAcc ±8 cm — your RTK fix isn't great right now (cell tower blocked? trees?). Don't trust depth tighter than 8 cm until it clears.
  • The glasses warn you before you commit to a bad number
This is the kind of save that pays for the system. One avoided over-dig = months of subscription.
GradeSight08

Bonus: paperwork is free

Your as-built builds itself.

Every time the operator marks a point done, GradeSight writes a row to a CSV:

  • Time, marker ID, label
  • Target depth vs. actual depth
  • Cut/fill in cm
  • RTK fix quality + accuracy
  • Lat/lon of the point and the operator

At the end of the day, download one file. Drop it in your permitting binder, your NRCS 580 closeout, your GIS layer.

What this saves you: a day or two of someone reconstructing as-built records from notebook scribbles. Every job. For free.
